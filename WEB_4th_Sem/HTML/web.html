Creation & Development

In 1962, following the success of the superhero team the Fantastic Four in comic books published by Marvel Comics, publisher Martin Goodman assigned writer-editor Stan Lee to create a new superhero team. Lee's wife suggested that he create a character with the powers of a spider. Lee then enlisted artist Steve Ditko to design the character. The duo collaborated on the first Spider-Man story, "Spider-Man. In his autobiography, Lee cites the non-superhuman pulp magzine crime fighter the spider as a great influence, and in a multitude of print and video interviews, Lee stated he was further inpired by seeing a spider climbs up a wall-adding in his autobiography that he was told story so often he has become unsure of whether he actually said it or not.
